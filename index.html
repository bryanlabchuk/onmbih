📖 How to Play

A guide to saving your haunted boyfriend

🎯 Overview

Your boyfriend Derek is possessed by seven generations of his ancestors' spirits. Your goal is to banish all seven spirits to free him from the family curse.

You'll need to research the spirits, recruit allies, upgrade your dice, and challenge each spirit to banish them.

🔄 Game Flow

1 🗺️
Travel

Use the map to visit locations around Blackwood. Each location offers different activities and bonuses.

2 👥
Recruit Allies

Meet townsfolk who can join your investigation. Each ally has unique abilities that modify your dice rolls.

3 📖
Research

Roll dice to gather research points. Research unlocks new dice upgrades and reveals spirit weaknesses.

4 ⚔️
Challenge Spirits

When you've gathered enough research, challenge a spirit. Meet their victory conditions to banish them!

🎲 Dice System

Dice are your primary tool for both research and spirit challenges.

Rolling

  • Click "Roll All" or click individual dice to roll them
  • You get 3 rolls per round
  • Shift+Click a die to lock it (it won't re-roll)

Scoring Bonuses

Certain combinations give bonus points:

Pair: +5 Two Pair: +15 Three of a Kind: +25 Full House: +35 Small Straight: +30 Large Straight: +45

Upgrading Dice

Spend research points to upgrade your dice:

  • Shapes - Unlock D8, D10, D12, and D20 dice
  • Colors - Colored dice provide contextual bonuses
  • Face Values - Increase individual face values
  • Modifiers - Add special effects like Reroll, Weighted, Clone, Exploding

📚 Research Milestones

As you accumulate research, you unlock new capabilities:

25 - Unlock colored dice (Red, Blue, Green)
50 - Unlock D8 dice
75 - Unlock Reroll and +1 modifiers
100 - Unlock D10 dice
150 - Unlock Purple and Gold colors
200 - Unlock Weighted and Clone modifiers
250 - Unlock D12 dice
500 - Unlock the legendary D20!

👥 Allies

Allies are townsfolk who help your investigation. Each has a unique ability:

  • Passive abilities - Always active (e.g., "+1 to all research rolls")
  • Triggered abilities - Activate on certain conditions (e.g., "On rolling a 6...")
  • Active abilities - Can be activated once per round

You can have up to 5 allies at a time. Choose wisely!

👻 Spirits

Seven ancestral spirits haunt Derek. Each has unique challenge conditions:

  • Score challenges - Roll a total above a target number
  • Pattern challenges - Roll specific combinations (pairs, straights)
  • Specific value challenges - Roll certain numbers

Defeating a spirit may unlock special dice faces as rewards!

🗺️ Locations

Each location in Blackwood offers different benefits:

  • Library - +50% research bonus
  • Occult Store - +1 spirit damage
  • Electronics Store - 20% dice upgrade discount
  • Soda Fountain - Restore sanity
  • Cemetery - Higher spirit encounter chance

Some locations are locked until you progress further in the game.

💡 Tips

  • Focus on research first to unlock dice upgrades before challenging spirits
  • Lock high-value dice to preserve good rolls
  • Match ally abilities to your play style
  • Location bonuses stack with ally abilities
  • Watch your sanity - if it reaches zero, it's game over!
  • Some spirits are weaker to certain colors or dice types
